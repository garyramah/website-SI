SUPERINTELIGENTNOST

Raziskovanje napredne inteligence. Inženiring odgovorne avtonomije.

Ime inštituta izhaja iz slovenske besede superinteligentnost — poimenovanja lastnosti, ne bitja. Pod okriljem družbe Agentics Inc. delujemo kot evropsko zasidran raziskovalni inštitut in specializiran inženirski laboratorij.

»Ne trdimo, da gradimo superinteligenco. Gradimo sisteme, ki organizacijam omogočajo, da vse zmogljivejšo umetno inteligenco uporabljajo varno, merljivo in pod lastno avtoriteto.«

Kaj pomeni superinteligentnost

Beseda superinteligentnost poimenuje lastnost — »biti superinteligenten«. Beseda superinteligenca pa poimenuje bitje ali sistem. Govorimo torej o lastnosti, ki se lahko uresniči v različnih nosilcih — in vprašanje je, kje.

Uresničena v enem samem računalniku

En sam, zaokrožen sistem, ki po splošnih kognitivnih sposobnostih bistveno presega najboljše človeške umove — na skoraj vseh področjih, od znanstvenega sklepanja in strateškega načrtovanja do ustvarjalnosti in razumevanja ljudi. Ne mreža in ne skupina sistemov.

Uresničena v umetnih oblikah

Lastnost, ki se pojavi na nebiološki podlagi: računalniki, programska oprema, roboti, porazdeljena omrežja, simulirani um. Izraz je širši od »umetne inteligence«, ker ne označuje ene same tehnologije.

Za razliko od bioloških ali kolektivnih oblik. Biološke poti — genetsko izbiranje ali izboljševanje, nootropiki, dolgoročno izboljševanje populacije z izobraževanjem, prehrano in zdravjem — ostajajo v okviru živih organizmov in so počasnejše: omejujejo jih biologija, velikost lobanje, hitrost nevronov in dolžina generacij. Kolektivne oblike — znanost, trg, internet — zmorejo to, česar noben posameznik ne bi mogel, a jih omejujejo komunikacija, usklajevanje in različni cilji udeležencev.

Ne glede na to, katera oblika se uresniči, je predmet inštituta enak: prehodi med agenti, orodji in podatki — tam, kjer dejanja postanejo posledice. Ovojnica ureja prehod, ne notranjost.

Samoizboljševanje

Sistem bi lahko izboljševal lastno zasnovo — eksplozija inteligence, ki jo je predlagal I. J. Good leta 1965.

Zakaj je to pomembno. Pri enem samem sistemu je vse osredotočeno: ena odločitev, en cilj, ena točka nadzora. V ospredju je zato vprašanje usklajenosti: ali so cilji takega sistema združljivi s človeškimi vrednotami? Če niso, lahko tudi navidezno nedolžen cilj privede do škodljivih posledic, saj sistem nima vgrajene težnje, da bi upošteval človeške interese.

Zakaj je ta opredelitev na tej strani? Ker inštitut ne gradi superinteligence, temveč meje za vse zmogljivejše sisteme — meja pa je smiselna le, če povemo, kaj leži onkraj nje.

SUPERINTELIGENTNOST

Researching Advanced Intelligence. Engineering Accountable Autonomy.

The institute takes its name from the Slovenian word superinteligentnost — the name of a property, not a being — and operates as a European-rooted research institute and specialist engineering laboratory under Agentics Inc.

“We do not claim to build superintelligence. We build the systems that let organizations use increasingly capable AI safely, measurably, and under their own authority.”

What superintelligence means

The word superinteligentnost names a property — “being superintelligent.” The word superinteligenca names a being or a system. So we are talking about a property that can be realized in different bearers — and the question is where.

Realized in a single computer

One single, rounded system that, across general cognitive abilities, substantially exceeds the best human minds — in almost every domain, from scientific reasoning and strategic planning to creativity and understanding people. Not a network, not a group of systems.

Realized in artificial forms

A property that appears on a non-biological substrate: computers, software, robots, distributed networks, a simulated mind. Broader than “artificial intelligence,” because it does not denote a single technology.

As opposed to biological or collective forms. Biological paths — genetic selection or enhancement, nootropics, long-term improvement of the population through education, nutrition, and health — remain within living organisms and are slower: bounded by biology, skull size, neuron speed, and generation length. Collective forms — science, markets, the internet — achieve what no individual could, but are bounded by communication, coordination, and the differing goals of participants.

Whatever form materializes, the institute's subject is the same: the crossings between agents, tools, and data — where actions become consequences. The envelope governs the crossing, not the interior.

Speed

Electronic circuits operate much faster than neurons.

Copying

A digital mind can be copied and run in parallel.

Scalability

It can be enlarged with hardware, which cannot be done with brains.

Self-improvement

A system could improve its own design — the intelligence explosion proposed by I. J. Good in 1965.

Why this matters. In a single system, everything is concentrated: one decision, one goal, one point of control. That puts the alignment problem front and center: are the goals of such a system compatible with human values? If not, even a seemingly innocent goal can lead to harmful consequences, since the system has no built-in tendency to respect human interests.

Why is this definition on this page? Because the institute does not build superintelligence — it builds boundaries for increasingly capable systems, and a boundary only makes sense if you say what lies beyond it.
